# 2.1.5 Circuit Simplification: De Morgan’s Theorems

## Overview

Augustus De Morgan developed two Boolean-algebra theorems that are essential for simplifying logic expressions and digital circuits. De Morgan’s theorems also provide the foundation for understanding NAND and NOR logic-gate equivalences.

In this activity, you will use De Morgan’s theorems along with other Boolean laws to simplify logic expressions and a digital logic circuit.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this activity, you should be able to:

- Simplify logic expressions using De Morgan’s theorems.
- Apply other Boolean laws during the simplification process.
- Write final expressions in Sum-of-Products (SOP) form.
- Simplify a digital logic circuit by simplifying its output expression.
- Draw equivalent AOI implementations using different gate constraints.
- Explain how to verify that two circuit implementations are equivalent.
