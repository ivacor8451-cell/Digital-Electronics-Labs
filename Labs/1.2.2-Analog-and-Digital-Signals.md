# 1.2.2 Analog and Digital Signals

## Overview

Although this is a digital electronics course, the world around us is analog. Digital electronic systems often monitor or control analog conditions, so effective digital designers must understand both analog and digital signals.

In this activity, you will analyze analog and digital oscilloscope signals and use the oscilloscope in Circuit Design Software (CDS) to measure amplitude, period, frequency, time high, time low, and duty cycle.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this activity, you should be able to:

- Differentiate between analog and digital signals.
- Determine amplitude, period, and frequency from an oscilloscope display.
- Determine time high, time low, and duty cycle for a digital signal.
- Use a simulated oscilloscope to make signal measurements.
- Compare measured and calculated signal values with circuit settings.

$$
f = \frac{1}{T}

$$
T = t_H + t_L
$$

$$
DC = \frac{t_H}{T} \times 100\%
$$
