# 1.2.6 Understanding Analog Design: The Random Number Generator

## Overview

Analog electronics is a distinct field within electronics. In this project, you will use Circuit Design Software (CDS) to build and test the analog section of the Random Number Generator (RNG).

You will observe a 555 timer oscillator whose frequency changes over time after the roll switch is released. You will use the oscilloscope to measure early, middle, and late output signals and connect those observations to the behavior of the RNG.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this project, you should be able to:

- Build and test the analog section of the Random Number Generator in CDS.
- Use an oscilloscope to observe and measure changing signals.
- Calculate frequency from a measured period.
- Explain how resistor and capacitor values affect oscillator frequency and timing.
